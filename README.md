### About Me

I'm an AI Engineer and published computer vision researcher with over 3 years
of experience in deep learning and production ML systems. My research focuses
on face verification using Siamese networks, transfer learning, and
contrastive learning. I also work with Large Language Models and
Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG). I like staying curious and learning new
things as the field grows, and I'm always up for the next interesting
problem — in research or in the real world.

### 📄 Featured Publication

> **Face recognition with the Siamese network on celebrity faces**
> Ali Habibi, Mahlagha Afrasiabi, Moniba Chaparian
> *Journal of Electrical and Computer Engineering Innovations (JECEI)*, Jan 2026
> Achieved **97.6%** verification accuracy on LFW and **96.25%** on ORL.
